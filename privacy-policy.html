Privacy Policy

Crusty Clipz does not collect, store, or sell personal data. The only data it accesses is the minimum required to authenticate with TikTok's API and publish video content (access tokens, open ID) to the account that explicitly authorized it. No data is shared with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely and only used to perform the posting actions you've authorized. You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings.
